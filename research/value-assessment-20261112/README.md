## The observational thread now has a precise obstacle

The [acceleration study](observations/research.md) uses the empirical radial acceleration relation, with its characteristic scale written as `a0(z) = A E(z)^q`, where `E = H/H0`. A constant scale has `q=0`. A Hubble-tracking scale has `q=1`. These are testable phenomenological alternatives, not complete gravity theories.

Let `b = B E^s` multiply the nominal baryonic acceleration and `k = C E^u` convert true total acceleration to measured acceleration. In the low-acceleration limit, the inferred slope combines `q + s + 2u`. Evolution of the physical scale and evolution of calibration therefore cannot be separated from that limit alone.

The full interpolation has an exact symmetry as well: shifting `(q,s,u)` to `(q+d,s+d,u-d)` leaves every predicted measured acceleration unchanged. Even adding a local, zero-redshift normalization leaves this drift ambiguity. This is a property of the declared inference model, not evidence that a particular survey has errors of the required size.

Independent calibration can break the ambiguity. The calculation gives conditional precision requirements and tests recovery with known calibration, acceleration-range holdouts, shared radial errors and a designed selection counterexample. It also shows why recasting the same masses and velocities as a Tully-Fisher relation does not supply independent evidence.

The literature and data-access review distinguish published fit summaries, parent survey cubes, best-fit catalogues and the selected sample's joint posteriors. A fresh individual-galaxy fit is not yet supported by the retrieved inputs. The next useful contribution is a reproducible, independently calibrated inference, rather than another curve through published bins.

## The geometry thread produces a narrower, tangible result

The [mechanics report](convergences/research.md) and [derivation](convergences/derivation.md) hold the original fifteen bars fixed as unstressed central springs. After internal relaxation, the open geometry supports three independent homogeneous stress combinations. A nonzero fold supports one. The surviving stress has mixed-sign principal values, so it is not an ordinary fluid-like pressure response.

Adding twenty-one explicitly declared weak springs removes the singular response at fixed positive added stiffness. Taking the fold to zero before removing those springs gives a different tensor from removing them first. Both the formula and the numerical controls are included.

The added springs have their rest lengths reset at each reference configuration. This is a comparison family, not a manufactured material following one unchanged interaction law. The result concerns homogeneous elasticity. The completed model's full spectrum and nonlinear stability remain untested.

Rank-one elasticity, framework flexibility and soft modes are established subjects. The project increment is the calculation for this particular fold and its stated weak-interaction completion. The [prior-art comparison](convergences/research.md) includes pentamode and unimode distinctions.

Historical priority of that result is unresolved. A physically specified crossover or load response would be worth testing. A claim of a new universality class would be premature.

## The collapse thread needs a better discriminator

The [caustic control](closure/research.md) begins with coherent cold infall. A pressure-over-density proxy rises by 5.08 times through the first caustic, even though every Fourier occupation remains conserved and no modes scatter into each other. Filtered density and stress approach the corresponding collisionless multistream solution as physical dispersion decreases.

This does not show that interacting AeST cannot develop an approximate pressure law. It shows that finite stress, smooth collapse and a rising proxy would not demonstrate such a law. Three admissible cold reference preparations also have identical density and zero local mean velocity while their stresses span a factor of four.

The finite next test in the report specifies the full action and branch, checks the canonical constraints, then measures density, current and stress responses. A large three-dimensional simulation is justified only after that test identifies physical states, retained variables and a controlled range below the effective-theory cutoff.

This connects the original question about what survives annihilation or collapse to a practical distinction: conserved charge, bound energy, collisionless stress and thermal pressure are different forms of persistence. The existence of one does not establish the others.
