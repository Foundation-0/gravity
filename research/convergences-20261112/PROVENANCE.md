## What the computations establish

The probes check mathematical consequences of stated models. They include symbolic identities, analytic limits, numerical convergence, independent formulations and synthetic inference examples. Reproduction checks verify the published artifacts under declared tolerances. They do not establish the physical correctness of the selected models.

The AeST compensated initial pair is a conditional algebraic witness derived from published linear variables. Its complete canonical initial-data map remains unimplemented. The collection also leaves nonlinear collapse, a healthy specified higher-derivative extension, stress lifetime, thermalization and a cosmologically selected pressure scale uncomputed.

The acceleration-evolution work compares published fitted summaries with a phenomenological model. It supplies no new galaxy-level likelihood or measured evolution exponent. Its source data and covariance requirements remain separate from the synthetic probes in this collection.

## Publication transformations

The public edition retains the nine reports, scientific source ledgers, original probe algorithms, generated results, selected derivations and independent review findings. Machine-specific paths become relative paths or immutable public source links. Exact scientific inputs needed for reproduction are bundled with origin and digest records.

Downloaded copies of third-party papers, temporary renderer output, private operational notes and machine-specific dispatch records are excluded. Their scientific citations and relevant findings remain in the reports. No personal memory store or private instructions are part of this collection.

Historical review hashes identify the pre-publication text reviewed during the research pass. Path and presentation edits change those bytes without turning the reviews into acceptance of later physical work. The publication validation separately checks the portable edition.
