# What becomes visible between the threads?

This research collection connects gravity, fractals, physics0 and astr0 through nine exploratory reports, primary-source ledgers and reproducible mathematical probes. It asks which patterns become informative when observations, disciplines and descriptions are combined. A shared fundamental theory is not assumed.

Prepared 12 November 2026. The first research passes are complete. Computational checks do not establish new physical laws or observational discoveries. [Provenance and reviewed revisions](PROVENANCE.md) records authorship, scope and source versions. [Reproduction instructions](VALIDATION.md) cover the executable probes.

**The most useful emerging distinction is between a thing, its relationships, and the evidence we can obtain about it.** Several examples show why those descriptions must be connected. They also show that joining descriptions is informative only when their differences are preserved.

## A different view can reveal what the first cannot separate

The evidence route gives an exact Gaussian example. A reference channel with no information about the target by itself measures a disturbance shared with the target channel. Joining them reduces the target's posterior variance by a factor of 25.75 under the stated model. Repeating the target channel a hundred times with the same shared disturbance barely helps.

This is an illustration of an established idea used in exoplanet searches, [half-sibling regression](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/scholkopf15.pdf). The signal-free reference assumption matters: a contaminated reference can subtract the wanted signal. More agreement among measurements with the same blind spot does not supply the missing view.

An astronomical example is concrete. DASCH's old observations of ASASSN-24fw support a long period but leave a half-period alternative open. ASAS covers the otherwise missing phase and shows normal brightness, excluding that alternative. [Published analysis, Section III.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.05367v3).

This suggests a practical search strategy across astr0 and physics0: look for the observation that makes the remaining explanations disagree. That may be an old plate, an uneventful exposure, a calibration reference or a spectral line excluded from the original fit.

## An unchanged local appearance can hide different relationships

The relations route constructs two quantum examples with the same reduced system state and the same complete dephasing channel. In one, any single environment qubit reveals a classical pointer bit. In the other, information is protected in joint correlations and every one- or two-qubit fragment is uninformative.

This connects fractals' Witness Map with physics0's quantum-error-correction interests. The example uses an established code, not a newly discovered natural environment.

A physical limiting case already appears in a paper cited by fractals: perfectly isotropic illumination can cause decoherence without producing redundant readable records. [Riedel and Zurek, Figure 2(b), Eqs. 17-18](https://arxiv.org/html/1001.3419v2).

The useful question becomes: what disappeared from the system's interference, what remains in correlations, and what can an observer actually read? Those are different quantities. A successful decoherence calculation does not alone determine all three.

## Geometry, interactions and measured patterns can change separately

The repetition route follows the existing incentre-lattice fold. Its original five-neighbor bar graph stays fixed as packing density changes. At the fcc endpoint, the full geometric contact graph has twelve neighbors.

A scalar-spin model with fixed coupling on the original bars is invariant by construction. Adding interactions at new contacts changes it. Even with unchanged graph-indexed spin correlations, a scattering pattern can change because physical positions enter the observation.

The convergence reviewer independently calculated vibrations of identical central springs on those bars. The spectrum changes as the structure folds, although the sum of squared frequencies stays fixed. A second spectral measure separates the geometries. This gives the spin-model comparison a mechanical counterpart.

The calculation fixes the periodic cell, uses motions repeated identically in each cell, and omits new contact springs. It does not establish the full mechanical stability or material properties of CaB6.

The unclaimed-connections route adds a different form of recurrence: a prescribed three-mass shape loop returns to its original internal shape while accumulating 27 degrees of orientation at zero total angular momentum. This is known geometric-phase mechanics. It is not a new free gravitational orbit. It suggests checking shape, orientation, relative phase and full state separately when a pattern appears to repeat.

## What remains depends on transport, history and the chosen boundary

The persistence route separates conserved excess, finite reaction exposure, protected configurations and continually rebuilt patterns. Its annihilation calculation shows how a symmetric residual and a conserved asymmetry are different survival mechanisms. Neither explains the origin of an initial asymmetry by itself.

The physical transport question crosses into gravity. Absence of electromagnetic radiation does not establish absence of energy export: known scalar-field models can eject field energy while leaving a bound object. [Seidel and Suen](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9309015). Whether a proposed AeST model does this requires its actual equations, fields and boundary conditions.

The unclaimed route adds angular momentum as a separate constraint. Cooling energy at fixed angular momentum does not permit arbitrary contraction. Local curl, phase winding, total angular momentum and a rotating figure also describe different things. Its wave examples distinguish them without asserting that the examples model AeST or real dark matter.

Persistence and visibility separate in observed astronomy too. HS Hya's binary continued to exist while its eclipses ceased as the orbital inclination changed. Historical plates and modern photometry preserve that history. [Davenport et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10954).

## A scale becomes more informative when we can predict how it moves

The scale route identifies several origins of a boundary: competing rates, accumulated history, an observational threshold or the failure of an approximation. Kibble-Zurek dynamics is an established bridge from finite-rate change to finite correlation length. It does not establish one universality class across galaxies, turbulence and decoherence.

The proposed galaxy-speed convergence needs further work. Its circular speed and one-dimensional dispersions are different observables, and its cooling calculation selects a gas density and an available time. These assumptions can move the apparent agreement. A useful next test predicts the full response to environment or epoch after definitions and inputs are fixed.

The independent review makes that test concrete. Under the notebook's fixed-overdensity, Hubble-time cooling assumptions, its cooling speed rises toward earlier epochs. The repository's approximate collapse speed falls. Their present-day proximity need not persist. These are conditional model responses, not new measurements.

The review also separates a density-profile slope from a stability threshold. The notebook's enclosed-mass formula is smooth through slope two. The proposed trapping onset is an additional assumption. A physical onset needs a growing perturbation or another dynamical criterion in the specified model.

These velocity scales are separate from the galactic acceleration parameter a₀.

The earlier [acceleration-evolution study](https://github.com/Foundation-0/gravity/blob/d8c7544e89388720fc1c13de816ddb7dd03d2096/acceleration-evolution/README.md) defines a₀(z)=A[H(z)/H₀]^q and compares its predictions with published observational summaries. It does not supply a new galaxy-data fit or a measured exponent.

## What we omit can return as a dependence on history

The hidden-dynamics route found a precise mathematical bridge. Start with two coupled quantities, then eliminate one exactly. Its influence can remain in a weighted history of the retained quantity and a term carrying the hidden initial condition. This is an elementary form of the established [Mori-Zwanzig method](https://math.berkeley.edu/~chorin/CHK02.pdf).

The probe reproduces the complete trajectory both ways. It also shows how adding a missing present variable can replace the history integral with a larger equation that uses only its present state. Whether a description needs history depends on what it retains.

A second probe keeps the same steady response while changing the hidden relaxation time. A changing input distinguishes the models. This supplies a concrete question for gravity and turbulence: which omitted variables matter for the timescale and observable we want to predict?

Readable records and returning influence remain separate. A departing photon can preserve a record without returning to affect its source. A small quantum swap example can return past influence after the system is reset. Neither an information record nor a history integral alone identifies a new fundamental force.

## The gravity question has a possible collapse experiment

[A proposed collapse experiment](https://github.com/Foundation-0/gravity/issues/5) asks whether nonlinear collapse in AeST can produce a persistent effective pressure from a dark field. That directly joins scale selection, energy transport, turbulence and the question of which variables suffice to predict a system's evolution.

In driven atomic-condensate experiments, large-scale shocks resemble those of a dissipative fluid. Conservative three-dimensional wave simulations reproduce the observations. Transverse instabilities feed vortex structures, offering a concrete route from resolved motion into smaller excitations. [Mossman et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/1712.06689).

That experiment concerns an atomic gas driven by a piston. It does not establish AeST dynamics or a cosmic pressure floor. It does show why a strict one-dimensional calculation could omit the mechanism one wants to test.

A complementary benchmark exposes a possible blind spot. A known perfect-caustic solution in a related ghost-condensate model has divergent curvature while the fourth spatial derivative vanishes. The proposed type of higher-derivative term supplies no restoring force for that configuration. [Arkani-Hamed et al., Section 3.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0507120).

This is a test case for the regularization, not an AeST impossibility result. The exact action, permitted initial states, boundary fluxes and status of the extra term must be specified. Long-lived velocity dispersion also does not alone establish thermalization or entropy production.

The route also derives a small diagnostic from the published linear AeST equations. Compensating a changed vector velocity with a changed scalar velocity preserves the initial effective density and velocity, while changing pressure and immediate acceleration. Independent algebra checks agree.

The complete canonical constraint map remains to be validated, and a restricted cosmological preparation could still permit an accurate fluid approximation.

The useful next step is to validate that initial-state construction in one specified linear model. Nonlinear tests can then measure retained stress, escaping energy and the response to a further disturbance. This could resolve part of the pressure proposal without claiming to explain a₀, inertia or the origin of the universe.
